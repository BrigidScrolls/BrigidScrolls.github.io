Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 8, 2025 (updated June 11, 2026)

Data Controller: Brigid Scrolls (sole proprietor, South Africa)

1. Overview

Scrolls MD is designed as an offline educational app that provides medical exam preparation materials. Biohazard Ward is a single-player offline horror game. Brigid Scrolls does not collect, store, or process any personal user data through our App, Game, or Website. However, certain limited data may be collected by third-party services integrated into the app, such as Google AdMob, which displays advertisements.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Scrolls MD App

We do not directly collect, store, or request any personal information. All in-app purchases are processed securely through the Google Play Billing system, and we never have access to your billing or payment details. The app itself does not collect any user data. However, Google AdMob may automatically collect limited, non-personal information such as device identifiers (for ad delivery and fraud prevention), approximate location (for regional ad relevance), and app usage statistics related to ad impressions or clicks. This data is collected and processed directly by Google, not by Brigid Scrolls.

2.2 Biohazard Ward Game

Completely offline. The game does not connect to the internet except for optional Steam/Itch.io features (if you purchase there). Save files are stored locally on your computer. Brigid Scrolls has no access to your save data. No telemetry, no analytics, no performance monitoring. Steam integration (if purchased there): Steam may collect data according to Valve's Privacy Policy (achievements, playtime, etc.). Brigid Scrolls does not receive this data.

2.3 Website (brigidscrolls.github.io)

We do not use analytics cookies or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages may collect standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, pages visited). Refer to GitHub's Privacy Policy for their practices.

2.4 Books (Amazon & other retailers)

We never receive your personal information from Amazon or other book retailers. If you email us about a book, we will only have the information you provide in that email.

2.5 Email Communications

If you email BrigidScrolls@Gmail.com, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We retain emails for as long as needed to resolve your inquiry, typically 90 days after resolution. We will never share your email address with third parties except as required by law.

3. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Scrolls MD only): AdMob may collect limited information automatically to serve relevant ads and prevent fraudulent activity. For details on how Google handles this data, please review Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can manage or opt out of personalized ads on your device: Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → Allow Apps to Request to Track. Brigid Scrolls has no access to or control over any data collected by AdMob.

Google Play Billing (Scrolls MD only): Processes all purchases securely. We never see your payment information.

Steam/Itch.io (Biohazard Ward, if purchased there): These platforms have their own privacy policies. Brigid Scrolls is not responsible for their data practices.

4. How We Use Information

Since Scrolls MD and Biohazard Ward do not collect personal information, we do not use your data for analytics, marketing, or tracking. Any limited information collected by AdMob is used exclusively by Google for ad-serving, performance measurement, and fraud prevention.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), data collected by AdMob is processed by Google under the following legal bases: Consent (for personalized ads) and Legitimate interest (for basic ad delivery and fraud detection). Users may withdraw consent for personalized ads at any time using their device's privacy settings.

6. POPIA Compliance (South Africa)

Brigid Scrolls adheres to the principles of the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), including: Data minimization — the app/game collects no personal data; Purpose limitation — any third-party data collection is used solely for advertising; Transparency — we clearly identify our third-party service (Google AdMob).

7. Your Rights

Under POPIA and GDPR, you may have the right to: Request confirmation of whether we process any personal data (we do not); Request correction or deletion of any personal data held by us (none is collected); Withdraw consent for third-party data collection (via device settings). Since Brigid Scrolls does not collect user data, most of these rights will apply to Google as the data processor for AdMob.

To exercise these rights, email BrigidScrolls@Gmail.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 15 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

Scrolls MD is intended for medical students and adult learners (13+). Biohazard Ward is intended for mature audiences (17+). We do not knowingly collect or process data from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided data through third-party systems, please contact us immediately.

9. Data Security

The Scrolls MD app and Biohazard Ward game do not store or transmit personal data. All purchases and advertisements are handled through secure third-party systems using encrypted communication channels.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The latest version will always be available at https://brigidscrolls.github.io/privacy-policy/. Material changes will be notified via website banner, in-app notification (Scrolls MD), or game update notes (Biohazard Ward).
