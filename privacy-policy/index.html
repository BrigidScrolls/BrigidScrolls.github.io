Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 8, 2025 (updated July 10, 2026)

Data Controller: Brigid Scrolls PYT LTD (South Africa)

1. Overview

Scrolls MD is designed as an offline educational app that provides medical exam preparation materials. Biohazard Ward is a single-player offline horror game. Patient 447 is a single-player offline horror game. Brigid Scrolls does not collect, store, or process any personal user data through our App, Game, or Website.

Exception: We operate an email newsletter to which you may voluntarily subscribe. When you subscribe, we collect and store your email address for the purpose of sending you updates. This is the only personal data we collect directly.

However, certain limited data may be collected by third-party services integrated into the app, such as Google AdMob, which displays advertisements.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Scrolls MD App

We do not directly collect, store, or request any personal information. All in-app purchases are processed securely through the Google Play Billing system, and we never have access to your billing or payment details. The app itself does not collect any user data. However, Google AdMob may automatically collect limited, non-personal information such as device identifiers (for ad delivery and fraud prevention), approximate location (for regional ad relevance), and app usage statistics related to ad impressions or clicks. This data is collected and processed directly by Google, not by Brigid Scrolls.

2.2 Biohazard Ward & Patient 447 Games

Completely offline. The games do not connect to the internet except for optional Steam/Itch.io features (if you purchase there). Save files are stored locally on your computer. Brigid Scrolls has no access to your save data. No telemetry, no analytics, no performance monitoring.

Patient 447: This game is distributed exclusively on Itch.io. Itch.io may collect data according to their Privacy Policy. Brigid Scrolls does not receive this data.

Steam integration (Biohazard Ward only, if purchased there): Steam may collect data according to Valve's Privacy Policy (achievements, playtime, etc.). Brigid Scrolls does not receive this data.

2.3 Website (brigidscrolls.github.io)

We do not use analytics cookies or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages may collect standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, pages visited). Refer to GitHub's Privacy Policy for their practices.

2.4 Books (Amazon & other retailers)

We never receive your personal information from Amazon or other book retailers. If you email us about a book, we will only have the information you provide in that email.

2.5 Email Communications (Support)

If you email BrigidScrolls@Gmail.com, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We retain emails for as long as needed to resolve your inquiry, typically 90 days after resolution. We will never share your email address with third parties except as required by law.

2.6 Email Newsletter

Brigid Scrolls operates an email newsletter to provide updates on game launches, book releases, and other company news.

What We Collect:

  • Email address (provided by you when you sign up)
  • Signup date and source (which page you signed up from)
  • We do not collect your name unless you voluntarily include it in the form

How We Collect It:

  • Through a Google Form embedded on our website (brigidscrolls.github.io)
  • You provide this information voluntarily
  • We do not use any automated signup or confirmation systems

How We Use It:

  • To send you occasional updates about Brigid Scrolls products
  • To notify you of new game releases (Biohazard Ward, Patient 447)
  • To notify you of new book publications
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your email with third parties
  • We do not use your email for any automated marketing

How We Store It:

  • Emails are collected and stored in Google Forms and Google Sheets
  • We manually export the email list from Google Sheets to send updates
  • We do not use any third-party email marketing platforms (e.g., Mailchimp, SendGrid)
  • Google's Privacy Policy applies to data stored on their servers: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How to Unsubscribe:

  • Email BrigidScrolls@Gmail.com with "UNSUBSCRIBE" in the subject line
  • We will remove you from our manual email list within 5 business days
  • We will not email you again after removal

Data Retention:

  • We retain your email address for as long as you remain subscribed to our newsletter
  • If you unsubscribe, we will delete your email address from our manual list within 5 business days
  • We may retain a record of your unsubscribe request for compliance purposes

3. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Scrolls MD only): AdMob may collect limited information automatically to serve relevant ads and prevent fraudulent activity. For details on how Google handles this data, please review Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can manage or opt out of personalized ads on your device: Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → Allow Apps to Request to Track. Brigid Scrolls has no access to or control over any data collected by AdMob.

Google Play Billing (Scrolls MD only): Processes all purchases securely. We never see your payment information.

Steam/Itch.io (Biohazard Ward & Patient 447, if purchased there): These platforms have their own privacy policies. Brigid Scrolls is not responsible for their data practices.

Google Forms & Google Sheets (Newsletter): We use Google Forms to collect email addresses and Google Sheets to store them. This data is stored on Google's servers and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Brigid Scrolls manually exports this data for sending updates and does not use any automated email marketing platforms.

4. How We Use Information

Since Scrolls MD and Biohazard Ward do not collect personal information, we do not use your data for analytics, marketing, or tracking. Any limited information collected by AdMob is used exclusively by Google for ad-serving, performance measurement, and fraud prevention.

Newsletter: Your email address is used only to send you the updates you have requested. We do not use it for any other purpose.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), data collected by AdMob is processed by Google under the following legal bases: Consent (for personalized ads) and Legitimate interest (for basic ad delivery and fraud detection). Users may withdraw consent for personalized ads at any time using their device's privacy settings.

Newsletter: We process your email address based on your consent (you voluntarily signed up via Google Form). You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing BrigidScrolls@Gmail.com with "UNSUBSCRIBE" in the subject line.

6. POPIA Compliance (South Africa)

Brigid Scrolls adheres to the principles of the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), including:

  • Data minimization — we collect only email addresses for the newsletter
  • Purpose limitation — data is used solely for sending updates
  • Transparency — we clearly identify our third-party services (Google AdMob, Google Forms, Google Sheets)
  • Accountability — we manually manage the email list and respond to unsubscribe requests within 5 business days

7. Your Rights

Under POPIA and GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether we process your personal data
  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your personal data (including email address)
  • Withdraw consent for third-party data collection (via device settings for AdMob)
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by emailing BrigidScrolls@Gmail.com with "UNSUBSCRIBE" in the subject line

Since Brigid Scrolls only collects email addresses for the newsletter, most of these rights apply to that data. To exercise these rights, email BrigidScrolls@Gmail.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 15 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

Scrolls MD is intended for medical students and adult learners (13+). Biohazard Ward and Patient 447 are intended for mature audiences (17+). We do not knowingly collect or process data from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided data through third-party systems, please contact us immediately.

9. Data Security

The Scrolls MD app and Biohazard Ward game do not store or transmit personal data. All purchases and advertisements are handled through secure third-party systems using encrypted communication channels.

Newsletter Data: Email addresses collected via Google Forms are stored on Google's secure servers. We manually export this data and store it securely on our devices. We do not share this data with any third party.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The latest version will always be available at https://brigidscrolls.github.io/privacy-policy/. Material changes will be notified via website banner, in-app notification (Scrolls MD), game update notes (Biohazard Ward), or email newsletter.
