1. Overview

Brigid Scrolls PYT LTD ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our products and services, including:

  • The Scrolls MD mobile application

  • Our website (brigidscrolls.github.io)

  • Any other products or services we offer

Key Principles:

  • We collect minimal personal data — only what you voluntarily provide
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data

  • We comply with all applicable privacy laws including POPIA, GDPR, CCPA, and PIPEDA

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Scrolls MD App

Scrolls MD is primarily offline. We do not directly collect, store, or request any personal information through the app.

However, the app requires internet connectivity for:

  • Ad delivery (Google AdMob) — if enabled
  • In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

All in-app purchases are processed securely through the Google Play Billing system. We never have access to your billing or payment details.

Google AdMob: AdMob may automatically collect limited, non-personal information including:

  • Device identifiers (for ad delivery and fraud prevention)
  • Approximate location (for regional ad relevance)
  • App usage statistics related to ad impressions or clicks

This data is collected and processed directly by Google, not by Brigid Scrolls. We have no access to this data.

2.2 Biohazard Ward, Patient 447 & Diagnostic Ward

All games are completely offline. They do not connect to the internet except for optional platform features (if you purchase there).

Save files are stored locally on your device. We have no access to your save data. No telemetry, no analytics, no performance monitoring.

Patient 447: Distributed exclusively on Itch.io. Itch.io may collect data according to their Privacy Policy. We do not receive this data.

Biohazard Ward: If purchased on Steam, Steam may collect data according to Valve's Privacy Policy (achievements, playtime, etc.). We do not receive this data.

Diagnostic Ward: This game is offline. No data is collected.

2.3 Website (brigidscrolls.github.io)

We do not use analytics cookies or tracking scripts.

However, GitHub Pages, which hosts our website, may collect standard server logs including:

  • IP address
  • Timestamp of visit
  • Pages visited
  • Browser type and version
  • Referrer URL

This data is collected and processed by GitHub in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement. Brigid Scrolls does not have access to this data and does not use it for any purpose.

2.4 Books (Amazon, IngramSpark & other retailers)

We never receive your personal information from Amazon, IngramSpark, or other book retailers.

If you email us about a book, we will only have the information you provide in that email.

2.5 Email Communications (Support)

If you email us at BrigidScrolls@Gmail.com, we receive:

  • Your email address
  • The content of your message
  • Any attachments you include

We retain emails for as long as needed to resolve your inquiry, typically 90 days after resolution.

We will never share your email address with third parties except as required by law.

2.6 Email Newsletter

Brigid Scrolls operates an email newsletter to provide updates on game launches, book releases, and other company news.

What We Collect:

How We Use It:

  • To send you occasional updates about Brigid Scrolls products
  • To notify you of new game releases (Biohazard Ward, Patient 447)
  • To notify you of new book publications
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your email with third parties
  • We do not use your email for any automated marketing

How We Store It:

  • We manually export the email list from Google Sheets to send updates
  • We do not use any third-party email marketing platforms (e.g., Mailchimp, SendGrid)
  • Google's Privacy Policy applies to data stored on their servers: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How to Unsubscribe:

  • Email BrigidScrolls@Gmail.com with "UNSUBSCRIBE" in the subject line

Data Retention:
