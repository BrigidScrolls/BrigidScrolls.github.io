Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 8, 2025 (updated July 10, 2026)

Data Controller: Brigid Scrolls PYT LTD (South Africa)

1. Overview

Scrolls MD is designed as an offline educational app that provides medical exam preparation materials. Biohazard Ward is a single-player offline horror game. Brigid Scrolls does not collect, store, or process any personal user data through our App, Game, or Website.

Exception: We operate an email newsletter to which you may voluntarily subscribe. When you subscribe, we collect and store your email address for the purpose of sending you updates. This is the only personal data we collect directly.

However, certain limited data may be collected by third-party services integrated into the app, such as Google AdMob, which displays advertisements.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Scrolls MD App

We do not directly collect, store, or request any personal information. All in-app purchases are processed securely through the Google Play Billing system, and we never have access to your billing or payment details. The app itself does not collect any user data. However, Google AdMob may automatically collect limited, non-personal information such as device identifiers (for ad delivery and fraud prevention), approximate location (for regional ad relevance), and app usage statistics related to ad impressions or clicks. This data is collected and processed directly by Google, not by Brigid Scrolls.

2.2 Biohazard Ward Game

Completely offline. The game does not connect to the internet except for optional Steam/Itch.io features (if you purchase there). Save files are stored locally on your computer. Brigid Scrolls has no access to your save data. No telemetry, no analytics, no performance monitoring. Steam integration (if purchased there): Steam may collect data according to Valve's Privacy Policy (achievements, playtime, etc.). Brigid Scrolls does not receive this data.

2.3 Website (brigidscrolls.github.io)

We do not use analytics cookies or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages may collect standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, pages visited). Refer to GitHub's Privacy Policy for their practices.

2.4 Books (Amazon & other retailers)

We never receive your personal information from Amazon or other book retailers. If you email us about a book, we will only have the information you provide in that email.

2.5 Email Communications (Support)

If you email BrigidScrolls@Gmail.com, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We retain emails for as long as needed to resolve your inquiry, typically 90 days after resolution. We will never share your email address with third parties except as required by law.

2.6 Email Newsletter

Brigid Scrolls operates an email newsletter to provide updates on game launches, book releases, and other company news.

What We Collect:

  • Email address (provided by you when you sign up)
  • Optional: First name (if you choose to provide it)
  • Signup date and source (which page you signed up from)

How We Collect It:

  • Through the signup form on our website (brigidscrolls.github.io)
  • You provide this information voluntarily

How We Use It:

  • To send you occasional updates about Brigid Scrolls products
  • To notify you of new game releases (Biohazard Ward, Patient 447)
  • To notify you of new book publications
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your email with third parties

How to Unsubscribe:

  • Every email we send contains an "unsubscribe" link
  • You can also email BrigidScrolls@Gmail.com with "UNSUBSCRIBE" in the subject line
  • We will remove you from our list within 5 business days

4. How We Use Information

Since Scrolls MD and Biohazard Ward do not collect personal information, we do not use your data for analytics, marketing, or tracking. Any limited information collected by AdMob is used exclusively by Google for ad-serving, performance measurement, and fraud prevention.

Newsletter: Your email address is used only to send you the updates you have requested. We do not use it for any other purpose.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), data collected by AdMob is processed by Google under the following legal bases: Consent (for personalized ads) and Legitimate interest (for basic ad delivery and fraud detection). Users may withdraw consent for personalized ads at any time using their device's privacy settings.

Newsletter: We process your email address based on your consent (you voluntarily signed up). You may withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing.

6. POPIA Compliance (South Africa)

Brigid Scrolls adheres to the principles of the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), including: Data minimization — we collect only email addresses for the newsletter; Purpose limitation — data is used solely for sending updates; Transparency — we clearly identify our third-party services (Google AdMob and our email provider).

7. Your Rights

Under POPIA and GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether we process your personal data
  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your personal data (including email address)
  • Withdraw consent for third-party data collection (via device settings for AdMob)
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in each email

Since Brigid Scrolls only collects email addresses for the newsletter, most of these rights apply to that data. To exercise these rights, email BrigidScrolls@Gmail.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 15 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

Scrolls MD is intended for medical students and adult learners (13+). Biohazard Ward and Patient 447 are intended for mature audiences (17+). We do not knowingly collect or process data from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided data through third-party systems, please contact us immediately.

9. Data Security

The Scrolls MD app and Biohazard Ward game do not store or transmit personal data. All purchases and advertisements are handled through secure third-party systems using encrypted communication channels. Newsletter data is stored with our email service provider using industry-standard security measures.
