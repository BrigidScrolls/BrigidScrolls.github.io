🎭 Welcome to Ward 7

What is Biohazard Ward?

Biohazard Ward is a psychological horror visual novel set in an isolated hospital ward. You play as a newly assigned doctor who discovers that patients aren't just dying — they're being erased.

How long is the game?

The main story takes 10-15 hours to complete, with 4 different endings based on your choices throughout the 14-day narrative.

What's included in the full game?

  • 14 Days of Horror – A complete narrative arc with rising tension and psychological dread
  • 4 Unique Endings – Your choices determine the fate of everyone in the hospital
  • 3 Character Memories – Unlockable backstories for Samir, Elena, and Marcus
  • Medical Diagnosis System – Identify real conditions from a database of 50+ illnesses
  • Load Shedding Events – Power cuts that plunge the ward into darkness
  • Dyslexia Mechanic – Your perception fractures as trauma accumulates
  • Complete Story – The truth about Patient 447, the benefactor, and Project Coast

📋 A 14-Day Nightmare

Each day brings new patients, new clues, and new reasons to be afraid. The truth unfolds slowly — but some doors can't be closed once opened.

🔬 Diagnose or Die

Use the diagnostic board to identify real medical conditions. Choose correctly or face the consequences.

🧠 Your Mind Is Breaking

As you witness more trauma, your perception fractures. Text becomes scrambled. Reality becomes unreliable. Trust nothing.

⚡ The Lights Go Out

Random power cuts plunge the ward into darkness. Patients panic. Equipment fails. You're alone with whatever is in the dark.

📖 Character Memories Unlockable

Unlock three complete backstory chapters exploring the pasts of Samir, Elena, and Marcus — why they stay, what they fear, and what they're hiding.

🎭 Your Choice Changes Everything

Multiple endings await. The story branches based on your diagnoses, your alliances, and the final decision you make when the truth is revealed.

What is the story of Biohazard Ward?

You are a junior doctor assigned to Ward 7 — a forgotten wing of an underfunded hospital. Your colleagues are tired. The patients are terrified. And no one will tell you why the lower level is off-limits.

As days pass, patients begin to disappear from the records. Not discharged. Not transferred. Just... gone. Your mentor warns you to stop asking questions. The night shift nurse slips you a key. And a patient in Room 447 keeps whispering the same phrase:

"You're one of us now."

Who are the characters in Biohazard Ward?

  • Dr. Samir Roy – Your mentor. He's seen too much. He knows more than he's saying. Memory Unlockable
  • Nurse Elena Vasquez – She has keys to every door. She's been waiting for someone to trust. Memory Unlockable
  • Dr. Marcus Webb – Friendly on the surface. But why does he avoid the lower level? Memory Unlockable
  • Patient 447 – A woman with no records. No family. No past. But she knows your name.
  • The Benefactor – He owns the hospital. He owns the staff. He owns you.
