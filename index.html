About Brigid Scrolls

What is Brigid Scrolls?

Brigid Scrolls is a medical education company on a mission to spread knowledge to everyone who seeks it. We believe that everyone should have access to quality information, especially when it comes to health and medicine.

What products does Brigid Scrolls offer?

We offer the Scrolls MD medical exam prep app, the Biohazard Ward psychological horror visual novel, and a comprehensive series of high-yield medical books for students worldwide.

Who is Scrolls MD for?

Scrolls MD is designed for medical students, doctors, and healthcare professionals preparing for USMLE, PLAB, NEET-PG, and other global medical licensing exams.

What is Biohazard Ward about?

Biohazard Ward is a psychological horror visual novel where you play as a junior doctor in an isolated hospital ward during a global pandemic. The game features 14 days of narrative, multiple endings, and unique diagnostic mechanics.
